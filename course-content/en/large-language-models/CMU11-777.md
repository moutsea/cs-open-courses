# CMU 11-777: Multimodal Machine Learning

## Course Overview

- University: Carnegie Mellon University
- Prerequisites: Not explicitly listed; the project work assumes deep learning, Transformer, and Python/PyTorch experience
- Programming Language: Python, PyTorch
- Course Difficulty: 🌟🌟🌟🌟
- Estimated Study Hours: 150 hours

CMU 11-777 studies how to integrate text, images, video, audio, and actions into shared representations and reasoning systems. The Fall 2024 edition runs from unimodal representations and fusion/fission through alignment and grounding, Transformers, generation, embodiment, and reasoning, with later sessions on interacting with vision-language models and agents. The reading list is a useful map of the field even if you only follow the public syllabus.

The course is project-based: 70% of the grade comes from a sequence of dataset, baseline, analysis, and final reports, with the remaining weight on paper readings and participation. That makes it a good fit for learners who already know the basics and want to build a multimodal system end to end; it is less suitable as a first deep-learning course.
