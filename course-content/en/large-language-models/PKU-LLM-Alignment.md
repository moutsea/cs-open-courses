# Peking University: Large Language Models and Alignment

## Course Overview

- University: Peking University
- Prerequisites: Not explicitly listed; students should be comfortable with deep learning, the Transformer architecture, and Python before attempting the assignments
- Programming Language: Python
- Course Difficulty: 🌟🌟🌟🌟
- Estimated Study Hours: 150 hours

This 2025 Spring course follows the main path of LLM development: pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning from human feedback. It covers the architecture and training of LLMs, inference and chain-of-thought prompting, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, reinforcement-learning fundamentals, policy optimization, RLHF, and direct preference optimization. Later lectures extend alignment to embodied and multimodal models, then move into NVIDIA training architecture, CUDA programming, and DPU programming.

The three homeworks are substantial. The first asks students to implement a BPE tokenizer, a decoder-only Transformer, the training loop, and LoRA fine-tuning. The second covers reward modelling and DPO. The third is a hands-on introduction to NVIDIA DPU programming. Lecture handouts and replays are public, and the course exposes the gap between alignment algorithms and the hardware stack that runs them.
