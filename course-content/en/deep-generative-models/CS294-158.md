# UC Berkeley CS294-158: Deep Unsupervised Learning

## Course Overview

- University: University of California, Berkeley
- Prerequisites: Significant experience with probability, optimisation, and deep learning
- Programming Language: Python, PyTorch
- Course Difficulty: 🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟
- Estimated Study Hours: 100+ hours

Berkeley CS294-158 studies deep generative models and self-supervised learning when labelled data is limited or unavailable. Topics include autoregressive models, flow models, latent-variable models, GANs, diffusion models, representation learning, and multimodal models, with selected material on large language models.

The course is strongly hands-on: homework asks learners to implement and train models, and the final project turns the methods into an independent research-style experiment. It is best suited to learners who already know deep learning and want to understand the practical details behind generative-model papers.
