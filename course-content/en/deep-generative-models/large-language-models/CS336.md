# Stanford CS336: Language Modeling from Scratch

## Course Overview

- University: Stanford University
- Prerequisites: Strong Python and PyTorch skills, probability, linear algebra, and basic systems knowledge
- Programming Language: Python, PyTorch, CUDA
- Course Difficulty: 🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟
- Estimated Study Hours: 150+ hours

Stanford CS336 takes the operating-systems-from-scratch approach to large language models. Instead of treating a pretrained model as a black box, students implement the main pieces of a language-model pipeline: data collection and cleaning, tokenisation, Transformer architecture, training and optimisation, evaluation, and post-training. The course is a particularly good fit for learners who want to understand why an LLM works at the level of algorithms, kernels, and systems rather than only calling an API.

The assignments are deliberately rigorous and require substantial engineering work. You should already be comfortable with PyTorch, numerical debugging, and the memory and performance constraints of modern hardware before starting.
