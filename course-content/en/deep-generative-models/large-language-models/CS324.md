# Stanford CS324: Large Language Models

## Course Overview

- University: Stanford University
- Prerequisites: Machine learning, natural language processing, and basic deep-learning experience
- Programming Language: Python
- Course Difficulty: 🌟🌟🌟🌟
- Estimated Study Hours: 80+ hours

Stanford CS324 is a broad introduction to large language models that connects modelling, theory, systems, applications, ethics, and societal impact. It is useful for building a map of the field: how foundation models are trained, how they are adapted and evaluated, what capabilities and limitations they exhibit, and what safety and governance questions follow from their deployment.

The course is an archived offering, so it should be read as a foundational survey rather than a current implementation guide. Its readings and hands-on exercises remain valuable when paired with a newer course such as CS336 or CS224G.
