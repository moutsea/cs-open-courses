# MIT 6.S978: Deep Generative Models

## Course Overview

- University: MIT
- Prerequisites: Graduate-level deep learning and either computer vision or natural-language processing
- Programming Language: Python, PyTorch
- Course Difficulty: 🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟
- Estimated Study Hours: 100+ hours

MIT 6.S978 is a graduate seminar on the theory, applications, and research frontiers of deep generative models. The syllabus covers variational autoencoders, autoregressive models, GANs, diffusion models, and applications in areas such as vision, robotics, biology, and materials science.

The course is research-oriented: students read papers, present work, complete problem sets, and carry out a final project. It is a good choice for learners who want a broad generative-modelling foundation beyond LLMs, but it is too advanced for a first deep-learning course.
