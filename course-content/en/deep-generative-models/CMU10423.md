# CMU 10-423/623/723: Generative AI

## Course Overview

- University: Carnegie Mellon University
- Prerequisites: Machine learning, deep learning, probability, and solid Python or PyTorch experience
- Programming Language: Python, PyTorch
- Course Difficulty: 🌟🌟🌟🌟
- Estimated Study Hours: 100+ hours

CMU's Generative AI course studies how generative models are built and scaled for text, code, images, and other data. The material connects autoregressive language models and Transformers with variational methods, diffusion, multimodal generation, evaluation, and the practical challenges of training models on large datasets.

It is a useful bridge between a general deep-learning course and a specialised LLM course. The assignments and project ask students to turn the mathematical ideas into working models, so it is best taken after learning backpropagation, optimisation, and basic neural-network architectures.
