# Princeton: Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency Technologies

## Descriptions

- Offered by: Princeton
- Prerequisites: Basic data structures, probability, and computer networking; no blockchain background required
- Difficulty: 🌟🌟🌟
- Class Hour: 20 hours

Developed by Arvind Narayanan and other Princeton instructors, this course explains Bitcoin and cryptocurrencies from a technical rather than an investment perspective. It begins with hash functions, digital signatures, and distributed consensus, then examines Bitcoin's decentralization, transactions and scripts, mining, anonymity, regulation, and alternative cryptocurrencies.

The accompanying textbook is freely available online, while the Coursera course provides videos, quizzes, and assignments organized into self-paced modules. The course is designed for learners who want to understand blockchain security models and system design; owning cryptocurrency is neither required nor recommended as part of the coursework.

## Topics

- Cryptographic hashes, hash pointers, and digital signatures
- Distributed consensus, proof of work, and incentives
- Bitcoin transactions, scripts, and blockchain data structures
- Mining, mining pools, and network security
- Anonymity, privacy, regulation, and alternative cryptocurrencies
